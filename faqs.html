FAQs

Your questions, beautifully answered.

Home / FAQs
Is treatment at Patil Dental Clinic painful?+

Absolutely not. We use modern anaesthesia and gentle techniques to ensure every treatment is comfortable and stress-free.

How long does a smile makeover take?+

Most smile makeovers are completed in 2–3 visits over 1–2 weeks, depending on complexity.

Do you offer EMI or payment plans?+

Yes, we offer flexible no-cost EMI options on most premium treatments.

Are walk-ins accepted?+

We highly recommend booking an appointment, but emergency walk-ins are always welcome.

Do you treat children?+

Yes — our paediatric dentistry suite is designed to make children feel calm and safe.

How do I book a consultation?+

You can book online through our Appointment page, call us directly, or message us on WhatsApp.

Is the clinic hygienic and sterile?+

We follow ISO-grade sterilisation protocols — every instrument is autoclaved and single-use where applicable.

Do you provide post-treatment follow-up?+

Yes, all treatments include complimentary follow-up visits to ensure perfect results.
